# Travel and Claims SOP

This standard operating procedure explains how to plan business travel and claim work-related expenses at Serimaju Technologies Sdn Bhd. It applies to all employees. Questions go to finance@serimaju.example.

## 1. Before you travel

1. Get your HOD's approval for the trip in the Kakitangan portal (Travel Request form).
2. For client visits, confirm the client's site rules with the account manager first. For example, Pelita Precision's Senai plant needs bookings at least 3 working days ahead, and Bank Teratai needs escorted access.
3. Book flights and hotels through the company travel desk (travel@serimaju.example) where possible.

## 2. Flights

- Economy class is the default for all employees.
- **Business class** is allowed only for flights **longer than 6 hours**, and only for **M grades (M1–M3) and above**.
- Book at least 14 days ahead where possible; last-minute bookings need a reason in the travel request.

## 3. Accommodation

| Location | Maximum per night |
|---|---|
| Kuala Lumpur, Penang, Johor Bahru | RM 350 |
| Elsewhere in Malaysia | RM 250 |
| Overseas | USD 180 |

Amounts above the cap need written HOD approval before booking.

## 4. Meals and daily allowance

- Overnight outstation travel within Malaysia: **RM 80 per day**.
- Overnight overseas travel: **USD 60 per day**.
- No meal allowance for same-day trips; claim actual business meals with clients under "Client entertainment" with receipts.

## 5. Ground transport

- Personal car: mileage at **RM 0.70 per km**, plus toll and parking with receipts.
- E-hailing (e.g. Grab) and taxis are allowed for client visits and airport transfers; keep the e-receipt.
- Office parking is not reimbursed.

## 6. Submitting a claim

1. Submit claims in the **Kakitangan portal** under *Claims*.
2. Attach a clear photo or PDF of every receipt.
3. Tag client-billable expenses with the **client project code**: `BTB-` (Bank Teratai Berhad), `PPM-` (Pelita Precision Manufacturing), `GCB-` (Gemilang Capital Berhad). Example: `BTB-0142`.
4. Submit within **30 days** of the expense. Claims submitted more than **60 days** after the expense date are **rejected**.

## 7. Approvals and payment

- Claims up to RM 2,000: approved by your immediate supervisor.
- Claims **over RM 2,000**: require approval from both the **HOD and Finance**.
- Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary if approved by the 20th of the month.

## 8. Medical and dental claims

Medical and dental claims follow the Employee Handbook (section 5), not this SOP.
